<!-- Content -->
<p>While I am trained as an anthropologist, I enjoy the challenge and rewards of working across different fields to take a more holistic approach to disaster risk management. I am currently a Post-Doctoral Research Fellow in the Department of Civil Engineering at the University of British Columbia (UBC), where I serve as Executive Director of the UBC <a href="https://drrn.ubc.ca/" target="_blank">Disaster Resilience Research Network</a> – a transdisciplinary group of researchers committed to inclusive and equitable disaster research. As a cultural anthropologist, I focus on heritage, the anthropology of space and place, and the anthropology of disasters. My ethnographic research in Vancouver, BC explores how building community and cultivating a sense of place can help people preserve what they value in the face of uncertain futures – highlighting strategies that communities can apply today to set the stage for future recovery. My past experience as a Fulbright scholar and heritage practitioner in Albania with the organization <a href="https://chwbalbania.org/" target="_blank">Cultural Heritage without Borders</a> taught me to appreciate the importance of everyday moments connecting people and place.</p>
